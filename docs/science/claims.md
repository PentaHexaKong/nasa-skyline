# Claims policy

What the app, the docs and anything written about the project may and may not say. Results are *geometric opportunities under stated assumptions*, not delivered power, a working radio link or guaranteed contact.

## Fine to say

- We compare terrain-dependent sunlight and Earth-visibility windows, and how they change across sites and dates.
- Results are *geometric opportunities under stated assumptions* (visibility rule, elevation margin, terrain resolution).
- Built on NASA LOLA terrain and JPL NAIF SPICE geometry (once true).
- A validation number, **only after** a matched comparison has actually been run (same period, same rule).

## Don't say

- "First" or "nobody else has this". STK, Quickmap and published research cover parts of it.
- Uninterrupted or guaranteed contact. Delivered power in watts. Battery survival.
- A required satellite count or a cost advantage.
- "Within X % of NASA" before the periods and definitions are matched. PGDA maps average 18.6 years and count any sliver of Earth.
- Queqiao-2's ~20 h figure as a validation target.
- Anything that implies a cause for real landing outcomes (IM-1, IM-2). We show conditions, not causes.

## Data credit

Wherever the app or docs present results, name every dataset with a direct link (see [data/sources.md](../data/sources.md)).
